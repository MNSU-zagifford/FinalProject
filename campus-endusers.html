Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my package?

Enter your tracking number in the tracking form and click "Track Package" to view the latest available delivery status.

What do the delivery statuses mean?

Received: Your package has arrived at the hub. Loaded: Your package has been loaded for delivery. En Route: Your package is on its way. Delivered: Your package has reached its destination.

Can I change my delivery preferences?

Use the Delivery Preferences section to choose a preferred delivery location. Saving changes requires a working connection to the CampusEco server.

What if my tracking number is not found?

Check that you entered the correct tracking number. If the problem continues, contact your campus delivery support team.
