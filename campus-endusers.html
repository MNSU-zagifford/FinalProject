❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my package?

Enter your tracking number (e.g. PKG10234) in the lookup form above and click "Track Package" to see your real-time delivery status.

What do the delivery statuses mean?

Received — Package arrived at the Ellis Hub. Loaded — Placed on an EcoCart. En Route — Cart is driving to your building. Delivered — Package left at your location.

Can I change my delivery preferences?

Yes — use the Delivery Preferences section above to pick a drop-off location and enable notifications at any time.

What if my tracking number is not found?

Double-check the number format (e.g. PKG10234). If the issue continues, contact your campus delivery team at the Central Hub.
